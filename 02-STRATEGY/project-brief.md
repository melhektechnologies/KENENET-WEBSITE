# Kenenét Consultancy — Project Brief

## 1. Verified Core Identity
- **Brand Name**: Kenenét Consultancy (with acute accent on the final "e")
- **Rejected Spellings**: "Kenent", "Kenenet"
- **Primary Tagline**: Transforming Workplaces.
- **Supporting Line**: Organizational Excellence for Sustainable Change.
- **Category**: Ethiopian organizational and management consultancy
- **Language**: English (at launch)
- **Primary Pages**: Maximum five primary pages (`/`, `/about`, `/services`, `/approach`, `/contact`)
- **Hosting Target**: Vercel

## 2. Brand Positioning & Visual Direction
- **Design Philosophy**: *"Quiet authority, with one warm signal."*
- **Primary Brand Colors**:
  - Brand Purple: `#7F3293` (Sampled from logo)
  - Brand Orange: `#F9A21B` (Sampled from logo)
  - Ink: `#1C1720`
  - Paper: `#FCFBF9`
  - Mist: `#F5F2F7`
- **Typography**:
  - Display: `Newsreader` (Editorial serif)
  - Body & UI: `Source Sans 3` (Humanist sans)
- **Signature Visual Element**: Hero headline: "Transforming" in roman ink, "Workplaces" in italic brand purple, and closing full stop in logo orange `.`.

## 3. Technology Stack
- **Framework**: Next.js (App Router), TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS
- **Icons**: Lucide Icons
- **Content Delivery**: Typed data layer (`src/data/`), strict gating of unconfirmed facts (no placeholders or invented stats).
