## 0. Read First

### 0.1 What this document can and cannot do

The repository (`melhektechnologies/KENENET-WEBSITE`) still shows a single commit, so the validation report's finding stands: the brochure, the newer company profile, the logo and the founder photo are empty files. The only verified facts available are:

- Brand name, tagline and supporting line
- Category: Ethiopian organizational and management consultancy
- ERP: provided through a technology partner, with implementation support
- Page structure, voice and audience

That means the **service catalogue, founder biography, company background, mission, vision, values, methodology and contact details cannot be written without invention.** This document does not invent them. Everything that can be written truthfully is written in full. Everything else is a precise `[CLIENT CONFIRMATION: …]` slot, and the copy around each slot is finished, so the page reads complete the moment the fact is inserted.

### 0.2 Legend

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Plain text | Publication-ready. Built only from confirmed facts, or from general explanation that makes no claim about Kenenét |
| `[CLIENT CONFIRMATION: …]` | A fact is missing. Nothing has been guessed. Publish nothing in its place |
| **Status** line | **Ready** · **Ready, wording approval** (built on confirmed facts, needs sign-off on phrasing) · **Blocked** (waiting for facts) |

## Appendix B: Style and Terminology

- **Brand name:** Kenenét Consultancy on first reference, Kenenét on later references. Always with the accent.
- **Tagline:** "Transforming Workplaces." keeps its full stop.
- **ERP:** always qualified by the partnership model in the same sentence, card or band. Never "our ERP".
- **Avoided phrases:** unlock your potential, revolutionize, cutting-edge, world-class, leading, end-to-end, trusted by, and any statistic, client count, award, certification or guarantee.
- **Voice:** short paragraphs, plain words, outcome-focused, organization-centred. Technical terms are explained once, in everyday language.
- **Repetition control:** the identity sentence appears in full only on Home and About, phrased differently in each. Closing bands use different headlines on every page.

## Appendix C: Drop-in Guides for the Blocked Sections

**Service module (use for every service)**
1. Name exactly as printed in the brochure.
2. 30 to 60 words in the brochure's meaning: what the service is, then what it involves, then who it is for only if the brochure says so.
3. No new deliverables, durations or outcomes.

**Grouping rule (apply only after the catalogue is transcribed)**
Use the brochure's own headings. If it has none, propose groupings by the kind of change a client is trying to make, label them proposed, and obtain approval before use. Flag overlapping services. Do not delete any approved service.

**Process phase module (3 to 6 phases)**
- **Number and name:** client's wording
- **Purpose:** one or two sentences, starting with what the phase achieves for the client
- **Key activities:** two to four short lines
- **What you receive:** one line

**Methodology interview (45 to 60 minutes, if the documents are silent)**
1. In plain words, how does an engagement begin, and what does the client experience first?
2. What are the phases, in order, and what does each aim to achieve?
3. What activities happen in each phase?
4. What does the client receive at the end of each phase?
5. How are the client's leadership and staff involved?
6. How is change sustained after the engagement?
7. Where does ERP implementation support fit, if at all?
8. What should visitors not assume about the process?
